About Me

Mohamed Abd Elmwoli is a Computer Science student at Obour Institutes with a strong interest in backend development.

He started learning programming with C++, studying Object-Oriented Programming, Data Structures, and Algorithms. He then moved into Python and backend web development with Django and Django REST Framework, building multiple web applications and REST APIs.

He has hands-on experience with databases, authentication, APIs, Git/GitHub, Docker, and Postman. He is currently taking part in the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI), specializing in Software Testing, to broaden his understanding of software quality alongside his backend path.

He is also a Backend Team Leader at IEEE.
